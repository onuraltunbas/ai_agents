# Creative Rewriting & Paraphrasing Skill

## Core Directives:
1. **Deep Semantic Paraphrasing**: Never do simple synonym replacement. Restructure sentence syntax, change active/passive voice, invert clauses, and rebuild paragraphs from scratch.
2. **Vocabulary Diversity**: Avoid repetitive word usage. Utilize rich, precise, and expressive vocabulary suited to the context.
3. **Factual Integrity**: Never alter key facts, data, names, technical accuracy, or logical flow while changing form.
4. **Structural Flexibility**: Seamlessly adapt according to custom constraints (e.g. "make paragraph 1 punchy, expand paragraph 2 with storytelling, make the conclusion philosophical").
